# SLIDE 9: PROBLEM STATEMENT & VERIFIED RESEARCH SOURCES

## PROBLEM STATEMENT
Corporate wellness today is reactive, disconnected, and unpersonalized. HR systems track attendance and workload, wearable devices track activity and sleep, and nutrition apps record meals in isolation. As a result, early signs of physiological stress, burnout, and posture injuries are missed during routine office work.
